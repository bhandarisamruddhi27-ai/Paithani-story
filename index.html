🦚 Weave Gatha

Every Paithani Has a Story

A student innovation project that connects traditional Paithani weaving with simple QR technology. The aim is to give every Paithani a digital identity and help customers understand the story behind it.

🌸 About Our Project

Weave Gatha is a concept for giving a digital identity to a traditional Paithani saree. Instead of knowing only the saree's design and price, a customer can scan a QR Code and learn about its identity, artisan, material, weaving process and heritage.

Traditional Paithani + QR Technology

🔎 Problem & Need

❗ Problems We Observed

  • Customers may not know the complete story of a Paithani.
  • Information about the artisan is often not available with the product.
  • It can be difficult for a customer to keep product details safely for future reference.
  • Traditional craftsmanship needs better digital recognition.

💡 Our Approach

  • Give each Paithani a unique identification number.
  • Connect the product with a QR Code.
  • Store important product and artisan information digitally.
  • Allow the customer to discover the Paithani's story by scanning.

💡 Our Innovation

“One Paithani – One Digital Identity”

Our idea is to connect every Paithani with a unique ID and QR Code. The QR Code can lead the customer to a digital page containing the product details, artisan information, weaving journey, certificate and cultural background.

🏛️ Heritage of Paithani

🌸 Paithani is a traditional handloom art associated with Maharashtra.

🌸 The art is traditionally associated with the historic town of Paithan.

🌸 Paithani is known for its silk, zari work and traditional motifs.

🌸 Peacock, lotus and other nature-inspired designs are commonly associated with Paithani.

🌸 Yeola is one of the important centres of Paithani production.

🌸 Through this project, we want to connect this traditional art with today's digital technology.

📖 Paithani Digital Identity

The QR Code connects the physical Paithani with its digital information. A customer can use it to view the product ID, weaving details, artisan story, certificate and heritage information.

📜 A Paithani With Trust

The project also includes a Certificate of Authenticity and a Thank You Card.

📜 Authentication Certificate

This certificate is designed to provide important information about the Paithani and its identity.

It includes details such as Saree ID, Silk Type, Zari Type, Weave Type and Origin.

The QR Code can also connect the certificate with the digital story of the Paithani.

❤️ Thank You Card

A special Thank You Card can be given along with the Paithani.

It expresses gratitude to customers for supporting Paithani artisans and their work.

The card also reminds customers that a Paithani represents traditional skill, culture and heritage.
