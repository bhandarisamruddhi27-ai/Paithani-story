🏛️ Heritage of Paithani

🌸 Paithani is one of the most precious traditional handloom arts of Maharashtra.

🌸 Its history can be traced back nearly 2000 years to the period of the Satavahana rulers.

🌸 The art of Paithani is traditionally associated with the historic city of Paithan.

🌸 Over centuries, Paithani became a symbol of royal elegance, patience and skilled craftsmanship.

🌸 Today, Yeola is one of the major centres of Paithani production, where this business is carried out on a large scale.

🌸 Every Paithani is therefore not just a saree but a living story of Maharashtra's cultural heritage.

❤️ Motive of This Project

The main motive of Weave Gatha is to preserve and promote the traditional art of Paithani.

We want to give recognition to the skilled artisans whose patience, creativity and dedication keep this beautiful tradition alive.

This project helps people understand that Paithani is not just a saree but a story of culture, history and craftsmanship.

Through QR technology, we aim to connect customers directly with the story and identity behind their Paithani.

Our vision is to protect Maharashtra's precious heritage and carry the beautiful tradition of Paithani forward to future generations.
