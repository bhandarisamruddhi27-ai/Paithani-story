🎨 The Design Story

The peacock is one of the most beautiful traditional motifs associated with Paithani.

Each motif reflects the creativity and detailed skill of the weaver who creates it patiently on the handloom.

👩‍🎨 The Artisan's Story

Behind every Paithani is a skilled artisan. Hours of patience, concentration and craftsmanship go into creating every design.

The hands that weave the threads are also weaving a piece of Maharashtra's cultural heritage.

This project is dedicated to every artisan whose hard work keeps the tradition of Paithani alive.

🏛️ Maharashtra's Heritage

Paithani is an important part of Maharashtra's rich handloom tradition.

Its traditional motifs, vibrant colours and intricate weaving represent the beauty of Maharashtra's culture.
